Services

Care you can rely on.

Minor surgical procedures

Minor and surgical procedures involve safe, simple operations performed on surface tissues using local anesthesia in a controlled clinical environment.

circumcision

We provide circumcision services carried out by qualified medical professionals in a controlled clinical setting, ensuring safety, hygiene, and patient well-being throughout the procedure.

Family/General Practice

Family and general practice services for all ages, offering acute, chronic, and follow-up care with full medical assessments, treatment plans, and specialist referrals when needed.

Screening and Medical Tests

Screening and medical tests are used to detect health conditions early, even before symptoms appear. They help identify non-communicable and chronic diseases, assist in diagnosis, guide treatment plans, and monitor overall health progress.

Specialist Referrals

A specialist referral is a request from a primary care doctor to a specialist for advanced medical care. It is used when a patient requires further expertise for diagnosis, treatment, or ongoing management of a condition.

Antenatal Clinic/Pregnancy Care

Antenatal care is healthcare provided during pregnancy from early stages until birth. It is a form of preventive care that supports the health of both mother and baby and should begin as soon as pregnancy is confirmed.

Ultrasound scans

An ultrasound scan is a non-invasive imaging procedure that uses sound waves to create images of internal organs. It is also known as sonography and is used to assist in diagnosis and medical monitoring.

Family Planning

Family planning services involve medical methods used to prevent pregnancy, space pregnancies, and support reproductive health. Options include contraception methods such as pills, condoms, IUDs, hormonal implants, and sterilization for men and women.

Optomertist

Our optometrist provides comprehensive eye care, diagnosing and treating common vision and eye health issues. When surgical treatment is required, patients are referred to an ophthalmologist or specialist surgeon.

Dentist

Our dental services focus on promoting good oral hygiene to prevent complications affecting the mouth and overall health. We diagnose and treat conditions of the teeth, gums, and mouth to ensure long-term dental wellness.

Laboratory services

Laboratory services involve testing clinical samples to assess a patient’s health. These tests help in the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of diseases, supporting accurate medical care.

Medical Infomation and checkups

We provide reliable medical information and advice through qualified healthcare professionals, ensuring accurate guidance tailored to your health needs. Consultations are available in person or via telehealth services.

Compassionate Care You Can Trust

Ladysmith Medical Centre is committed to providing high-quality, patient-centered healthcare to the community of Ladysmith and surrounding areas. Founded in 2010, our practice has grown with a strong focus on compassionate care, professionalism, and trust.

He obtained his MBChB degree from the University of Natal in 2003 and has since dedicated his career to delivering reliable and accessible medical care. Health Insights

💧 Hydration Supports Vital Function

The human body is made up of approximately 60% water. Staying hydrated helps regulate body temperature, supports organ function, and improves energy levels throughout the day.

🥗 Nutrition Impacts Long-Term Health

A balanced diet reduces the risk of chronic diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, and heart disease. Proper nutrition strengthens the immune system and supports overall well-being.

🏃 Physical Activity Protects the Heart

Regular exercise improves cardiovascular health, reduces stress, and helps maintain a healthy weight. Even 30 minutes of daily movement can significantly improve long-term health outcomes.

🩺 Early Detection Saves Lives

Routine medical check-ups help identify health issues before symptoms appear. Early diagnosis often leads to more effective treatment and better recovery rates.
